About this archive

Every entry answers one question: why does this detail exist? Sources are museum records (Smithsonian, The Met, CC0), the 1945 Quartermaster jungle-uniform study, and standard uniform references. Photography is CC0 / public domain.

Confidence levels. Every card carries one: established (primary or institutional evidence plus corroboration), probable (strong but single-source or inferential), unresolved (sources conflict, or the story is legend). We do not upgrade a claim for narrative neatness — if a date rests on one authority, the card says so.

garmentsucks is an anonymous research project. No ads, no trackers.
