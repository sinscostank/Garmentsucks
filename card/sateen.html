Sateen: the wrong cloth for the jungle

A wind fabric, not a heat fabric. Dense, warm, slow to dry.

established

The M-1943 field jacket used a close, wind-resistant cotton sateen in a darkened olive — excellent for a European winter fought in layers, a liability in the tropics. The tight face blocks air movement and holds water. This is the clearest example of a correct answer to the wrong climate: the Army kept issuing light HBT in the jungle instead.
