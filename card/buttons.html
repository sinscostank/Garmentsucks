Buttons, not zippers — and the legend

The famous WWI zipper story is folklore. The design logic is real.

probable

The tale says zippers jammed on mud and blood and were abandoned after WWI. The zipper was not mainstream until the 1930s, so mass zippered WWI uniforms are unlikely; treat the story as legend. What the records do show: the tropical jacket kept a button front and, from the second pattern, hid the buttons behind flaps — buttons don't corrode in monsoon humidity and can be re-sewn in the field.
