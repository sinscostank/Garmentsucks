Hysteric Glamour: repro, then lighten it

Reproduce the spec cloth, then drop the weight for daily wear.

probable

Hysteric reproduces military-spec materials — an air-force nylon twill MA-1, a third-pattern fatigue shirt — then rebalances them. Its HG1963 fatigue shirt keeps the four flap pockets but uses an 80/20 cotton-weather cloth so it layers like a shirt.
