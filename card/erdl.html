history · 1948

ERDL waited nineteen years

Drawn in 1948, issued in 1967.

probable

The four-colour leaf-and-twig pattern was developed at ERDL in 1948 and shelved. Solid OG-107 persisted until Vietnam, where a single dark green on wet cloth reads as one dark mass. Accounts place ERDL issue to recon and special operations from 1967, USMC adoption in 1968, and its evolution into M81 woodland in 1981.
