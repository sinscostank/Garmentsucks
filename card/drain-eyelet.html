A pocket with a hole in the bottom

The function is clear. Which patterns carried it is not.

unresolved

A bellows pocket in a monsoon holds water, and the eyelet lets it out. The idea is older than the jacket — WWI gas-mask bags carried brass drain grommets. But which jungle-jacket patterns had the holes is disputed: collectors record them on the first and fourth patterns, say the third omitted them, and disagree about the second. Publish the function, not the pattern history, until an object-level check settles it.
