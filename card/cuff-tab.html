closure · 1943

The wrist is a valve

Sealed against rain and brush; opened for air.

probable

Buttoned cuffs keep wind, rain, insects and thorns out. Opened, they ventilate and let the sleeve roll on a gusset. Military field jackets carried the buttoned cuff from the M-1943 through the M-51; the M-65 moved to Velcro. The jungle jacket used an adjustable button closure at the cuff — look for one button and a short tab at the sleeve end.
