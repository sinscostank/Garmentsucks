fabric · 1943

Ventile: waterproof without a membrane

Extra-long-staple cotton, woven so tightly that wet fibres swell shut.

established

Developed at the Shirley Institute in 1943 for RAF airmen, Ventile uses low-twist ELS cotton in a high-density weave. Dry, it breathes; wet, the fibres swell and close the pores. No coating, no plastic membrane — a different answer to wind and water without a sweatbox.
