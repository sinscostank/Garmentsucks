Yohji Yamamoto: drape as deconstruction

Gabardine, raw-cut edges, volume that falls away from the body.

probable

Yohji's military references are built on high-density cotton and wool gabardine, cut oversized so the cloth falls away from the shoulder, with raw-cut edges used as ornament. Y's Resort 2023 turned cotton-twill cut edges into decoration and replaced structured canvas with soft linen.
