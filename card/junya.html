Junya Watanabe: upgrade the archetype

Hybrids, reversibles, and technical membranes over military cloth.

probable

Junya treats utility as raw material: a reversible MA-1/denim hybrid, a parka/cape silhouette, and an M-65 upgraded with GORE-TEX and Dyneema. FIT's museum describes his codes as exposed raw seams, cut-out darts, and garment insides turned into details.
