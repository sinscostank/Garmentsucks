Mihara Yasuhiro: docking and sun-fade

Two garments stitched together, then faded like a field jacket.

probable

Mihara's signature is assembly: denim and MA-1 stitched front-to-back, details 'docked' onto a shell, garments stripped of their original purpose. His sun-faded cotton military jacket uses finish — not print — to signal age.
