The safari belt is a hinge between function and fiction

It holds loaded pockets up — and it may be theatre.

unresolved

The belted bush jacket descends from khaki-drill uniforms of the Boer War, and the belt transfers loaded-pocket weight to the hips. But the real ammunition weight rode on a separate cartridge belt, and some outfitters call the self-belt pure fashion: 'no one uses the belt in the bush.' The practical-versus-decorative question is genuinely disputed.
