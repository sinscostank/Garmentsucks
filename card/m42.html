history · 1942

The jungle jacket's accidental ancestor

It was built for men who jumped out of aircraft.

probable

The M-1942 jump jacket answered one question: what does a man need landing behind enemy lines and fighting alone? Four cargo pockets, a pleated action back, gussets, a waist belt, split cuffs — and thin poplin, because weight kills a jumper. Museum and dealer records agree on the features; the wider lineage is inference.
