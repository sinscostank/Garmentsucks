Red Kap: the baseline everyone dismantles

Spec-sheet workwear — the control group.

established

Red Kap builds to spec: 7.5 oz poly-cotton twill, 5 oz poplin, 4.25 oz ripstop, bartacked pockets, action-back pleats. It produced combat fatigues and field jackets during WWII. It is useful precisely because it is un-deconstructed: the fashion labels keep its pocket architecture and remove its stiffness. Compare a bartacked pocket on a $60 work shirt with the same construction on a $400 jacket — the stitch pattern is identical.
