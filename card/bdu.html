The late answer: durability over comfort

NYCO ripstop, 1981 — the tropical jacket's compromise era.

established

The BDU traded pure cotton comfort for a nylon-cotton blend that dries faster and resists abrasion. It is the end point of the story that starts with khaki: each generation optimises for a different threat — heat, wear, or cost.
