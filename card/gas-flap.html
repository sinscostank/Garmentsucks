The ghost of Ypres

An inner strip behind the buttons, meant to keep gas out.

probable

The gas flap is a second internal door behind the placket, sizing the gaps between buttons. It traces to WWI chemical warfare — a 1918 British officer's jacket carries one at the collar. Collectors record an inner gas flap on early Vietnam tropical jackets and its removal on the third pattern. Institutional confirmation of the exact pattern is still open.
