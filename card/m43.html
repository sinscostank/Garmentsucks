The right answer to the wrong question

M-1943 was a layering system for a European winter.

probable

Cut loose to stack wool and a liner, with a wind-resistant sateen shell, the M-1943 was a system, not a coat. Its virtues were all cold-weather virtues, which is why it could not simply be reissued in the jungle. The Army kept issuing light HBT there instead.
